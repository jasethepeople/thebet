The Bet · crew is the running scoreboard for every bet your friend group already makes.

Create a bet in 10 seconds. Your crew votes. Loser does the forfeit. Winners earn BB — bragging bucks.

BB has no cash value — just bragging rights.

18+ only. Zero tolerance for harassment or sexual content involving minors.
Custom forfeits are filtered and can be reported.
