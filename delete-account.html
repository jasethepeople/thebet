Delete your account

Deleting your account permanently removes your display name, email, crew memberships, bets, picks, BB balance, and Pro status. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

To delete your account, email jasonclarkagain@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Bet account”. Include your account email in the message. We will confirm by reply once your account and associated data are deleted.

Download your data

You can get a copy of the data tied to your account: display name, email, crew memberships, bets created, picks, BB balance, and Pro status.

To download your data, email jasonclarkagain@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Export my Bet data”. Include your account email in the message. We will send you a copy of your data by reply.
