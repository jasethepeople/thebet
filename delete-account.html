Support: support@thebet.website

Request deletion or data export

Delete your account

Deleting your account permanently removes your display name, email, crew memberships, bets, picks, BB balance, and Pro status. Deleted data cannot be recovered. Use the support form above — pick "Delete my account" — and we will confirm by reply once your account and associated data are deleted.

Download your data

You can get a copy of the data tied to your account: display name, email, crew memberships, bets created, picks, BB balance, and Pro status. Use the support form above — pick "Download a copy of my data" — and we will send you a copy by reply.

Contact

Prefer email? Write to thebetwebsite@gmail.com from the email address on your account.
