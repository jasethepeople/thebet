The Bet · crew — Privacy & Content Policy

The Bet does not allow real-money gambling. BB (bragging bucks) have no monetary value: they cannot be bought for cash, cashed out, or traded. The only purchase is an optional Pro feature unlock.

Content Policy

The Bet is 18+ only. We do not allow content that sexualizes minors, non-consensual behavior, threats, harassment, or doxxing. All user-generated forfeits are filtered before posting; blocked content is not stored. Users can report any bet or forfeit from the app (the Report link on every bet), and reported content is reviewed and removed if it violates this policy. Content that sexualizes minors is reported to the NCMEC CyberTipline as required by US law.

Data We Collect

Display name, email (for sign-in), crew memberships, bets created, and picks. No location tracking, no contacts upload, no ad identifiers.

Contact

Appeals, reports, and questions: report@thebet.website
